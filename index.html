🏢 Inside Intuit
🆓
Internal Launch

QB Free Programmatic Campaign Goes Live on DV360

The Programmatic team launched its first-ever media campaign supporting the new QuickBooks Free SKU. Running on DV360 with Display (video on the horizon), the $250K campaign targets Custom Intent segments for SMBs, Solopreneurs, and Early Start businesses. The flight runs 4/9–4/30 via Initiative/Kinesso. Learnings will directly inform the monetization and growth blueprint heading into Q4.

🧪
Internal Test

CM360 Creative Optimization A/B Test Now Live in DV360 Prospecting

The team launched a $45K A/B test evaluating CM360 Optimized Rotation vs. Even Rotation across DV360 Prospecting (Display + Video), running 4/6–4/30. The test uses ML-driven creative weighting optimized toward Engaged Site Visit, with a success threshold of 15% improvement in CPeSV. If it wins, Optimized Rotation could become the new default for Standard Display + Video creative management at QuickBooks.

Deep Dive · QB

Amazon DSP Value-Based Bidding A/B Test Underway

QuickBooks is an initial beta tester for Amazon's newly introduced Value-Based Bidding (VBB) — a methodology that incorporates conversion revenue (based on average LTR) into automated bid optimization, reducing reliance on manual adjustments. The $240K A/B test (3/27–4/30) splits Amazon 2P in-market audiences across the current CPA/conversion-volume model vs. the new VBB approach. Intuit QB has been credited as a key driver in getting this capability developed. A positive outcome would align Amazon's setup with existing VBB configurations already live in DV360 and The Trade Desk.

🎯
Deep Dive · QB Prospecting

Programmatic Scale & Efficiency: CPeSV Holding Strong at $7.25

QB MoF Prospecting programmatic spend grew 18% WoW with CPeSV steady at $7.25 — well below the $14 benchmark. Traffic quality spiked significantly, driving an 18% increase in Engaged Site Visits despite a slight dip in overall visit volume. The team is actively shifting budget away from high-cost competitor audiences (which spiked to $43.56 CPeSV) and reallocating to higher-efficiency segments like Amazon Seller Central Lookalikes, delivering a standout $1.57 CPeSV.

🔄
Deep Dive · MC Programmatic

Mailchimp Prog Structure Revert Stabilizes Performance Across All Tactics

Following a structural revert on 4/13, Mailchimp's programmatic campaigns have stabilized back to historic levels with every tactic moving in the right direction simultaneously. Demand Gen MOF recorded its highest CPeSV QTD, while Display BOF led efficiency with CPM down 70% and CPPSU down 60% vs. Week 35 — at higher investment levels. The consolidation exit from learning phase has also allowed video investment to shift into display inventory, improving overall campaign efficiency heading into Q4.
